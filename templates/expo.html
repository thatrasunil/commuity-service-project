AI-Based Low Crop Yield Prediction & Smart Advisory System

Transforming traditional agriculture guesswork into precision intelligence. Predicting crop harvest 3 months before harvest time and providing farmers with actionable fertilizer prescriptions to prevent devastating crop losses.

Why Was This Project Built? (The Problem & Solution)

Understanding the agricultural crisis in India and how Artificial Intelligence provides a lifesaver for smallholder farmers.

1. The Crisis of Guesswork
Over 60% of Indian farmers rely on ancestral intuition or uncalibrated chemical fertilizers. Unpredictable monsoons, nutrient-depleted soil, and erratic weather often cause sudden 40-60% yield drops. By the time harvesting arrives, the financial loss is irreversible.
2. AI-Powered Early Warning
AgriVision AI analyzes soil fertility ($N, P, K, pH$), local rainfall, and temperature using a Random Forest Regressor (99.57% accuracy). It predicts the expected harvest in Tonnes/Hectare months before harvest, alerting the farmer if their crop is at risk.
3. Exact Farm Prescription
Instead of giving vague advice, the system generates a concrete shopping list of fertilizer bags (Urea, DAP, MOP) and soil amendments (Lime for acidic soils). This optimizes farming expenses, prevents fertilizer waste, and directly maximizes net farm income.

How The System Works: 4-Step Technical Architecture

End-to-end data pipeline from farmer field parameters to real-time machine learning inference and smart advisory:

Step 1
Farmer Data Input

Farmer inputs Crop type, Soil N-P-K nutrient values, Soil pH, rainfall, and farm size in Acres.

Step 2
Data Preprocessing

ColumnTransformer applies StandardScaler on climate/soil numbers and OneHotEncoder on categorical fields.

Step 3
Random Forest Engine

160 Decision Trees predict the exact harvest yield in Tonnes/ha and calculate the Agronomic Stress Index.

Step 4
Farm Plan & Audio

Nutrient gap analysis outputs exact Urea/DAP bag counts, harvest value in ₹ INR, and speaks advice aloud in English/Hindi.

Project Expo Viva & Evaluation Q&A Cheat Sheet For Evaluators & Students
Q1: What is the main objective of this project?
Answer: The main objective is to eliminate guesswork in agriculture by using Machine Learning to predict crop yield before harvest, detect early crop stress, and provide farmers with an actionable, tailored fertilizer and soil management plan to prevent financial losses.
Q2: Why did you choose Random Forest Regressor over Linear Regression?
Answer: Agricultural yield is inherently non-linear and governed by complex biological curves (e.g. too little Nitrogen wilts the plant, too much Nitrogen causes lodging). While Linear Regression achieved 97.14% R² with high error (1.28 t/ha), Random Forest achieved 99.57% R² with only 0.39 t/ha error by building 160 decision trees that capture complex non-linear patterns without overfitting.
Q3: How does the Smart Advisory Engine calculate fertilizer bag requirements?
Answer: The engine performs a nutrient gap analysis against official ICAR (Indian Council of Agricultural Research) benchmark ranges. If Nitrogen is deficient, it computes the exact elemental deficit in kg/ha, scales it to the farmer's acreage, and converts it into standard commercial 50kg bags of Urea (46% Nitrogen), DAP (46% Phosphorus), and MOP (60% Potassium).
Q4: How does the system cater to elderly or illiterate farmers?
Answer: The system features a Simple Farmer Mode with 1-tap visual crop cards, color-coded traffic-light status badges, a font size scaler, and a bilingual AI Voice Assistant that speaks the advisory aloud in both English and Hindi.
