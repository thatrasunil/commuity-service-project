Why Random Forest Won Over Linear Regression:

Crops do not grow in a straight line. If you give a plant too little Nitrogen, it wilts; if you give it too much, it falls over. Linear Regression cannot understand this curve (1.28 t/ha error), whereas Random Forest uses 160 decision trees to master non-linear climate and soil rules (0.39 t/ha error).
